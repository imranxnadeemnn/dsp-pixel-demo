🧩 RZR App2Web Pixel Generator

For Sales / Customer Success. Builds the advertiser's landing URL and the copy-paste snippets for the production pixel rzr_beat.js. Fill the fields — snippets update live.

1) Landing page URL (put this in the campaign)

RZR appends the click id at serve time. The pixel reads ck_id from the URL and attaches it to every event.

{CLICK_ID} is RZR's serve-time click macro (Encore substitutes it). utm_source=aarki is always added.

2) Base pixel — paste in the site <head> (all pages)

Loads the pixel and fires pageview. In SPAs, also call rzr_beat.fire("pageview") on each route change.

3) Consent — wire to the cookie banner (required)

The pixel stays silent until consent is granted; events fired earlier are queued and flushed on grant.

4) Conversion event tag — paste on the conversion page/action

Leave a field blank to omit it. For runtime values (cart total, order id), replace the literal with your dataLayer variable.
